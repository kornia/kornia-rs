`kornia_3d::pnp::refine_pose_lm` (used by EPnP with `refine_lm`, `solve_pnp_ransac` and the Python `solve_pnp_ransac`) now runs a fixed-size 6-DoF Levenberg-Marquardt loop with its normal equations on the stack instead of the generic factor-graph solver. It makes 2 heap allocations per call whatever the number of points or iterations, against 384 for 10 points and 2,749 for 100 points before. Results agree with the previous solver to within about 2e-4 px RMSE but are not bit-identical.
