Single-channel `pyrdown_f32` and `scharr_spatial_gradient_float` evaluate their interiors 8 lanes at a time on kornia-algebra's `simd::F32x8`, bit-identical to the previous per-pixel loops (no FMA). On 752x480 images (one core, criterion, best of 3 interleaved rounds): `pyrdown_f32` 2.53 → 1.29 ms (1.95x), Scharr dx+dy 3.57 → 1.17 ms (3.05x).
