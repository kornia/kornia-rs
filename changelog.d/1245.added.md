`kornia_3d::depth::DepthImage`: a `u16` or `f32` depth image with its `meter` (depth units per metre, rerun's convention: `1000.0` for millimetres, `1.0` for metres), validated once at construction (finite, positive, finite `1 / meter`; for `u16` also `65535 · (1 / meter)` finite). `DepthImage::to_pointcloud` / `to_pointcloud_into` turn it and `PinholeCamera` intrinsics into a `PointCloud`, skipping masked pixels and missing samples (same rule as `kornia_imgproc::depth`: `u16` valid iff non-zero, `f32` valid iff finite and positive), optionally with each point's source pixel index; the `_into` form reuses the caller's buffers. Checked against Open3D's `create_from_depth_image`.
