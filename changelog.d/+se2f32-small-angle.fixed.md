`SE2F32` is now accurate for small rotations. Below 1e-6 rad, `right_jacobian` and `left_jacobian` returned the third column `[p2, -p1]`, where the limit is `[-p2/2, p1/2]` (right) and `[p2/2, -p1/2]` (left). Just above that limit branch they returned values off by up to a few hundred (at 1e-5 rad), from f32 cancellation. `exp` and `log` dropped the `(1 − cos θ)/θ` term below ~3e-4 rad, so their translation was off by up to ~1e-4. All four now use Taylor series below 0.1 rad; results above 0.1 rad are unchanged.
