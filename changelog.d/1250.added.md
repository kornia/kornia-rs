CUDA FAST-N corner detector written in Rust with cuda-oxide (`cuda::fast::fast_detect_u8`, `launch_fast_score_u8`), bit-exact with `fast_detect_rows_u8` apart from the CPU's in-block local-max filter at widths of 800 and above. On an RTX 2050 the kernel alone runs 2.8-3.5x faster than the 12-thread CPU path; a standalone call including host-device transfers is roughly break-even (0.5-1.0x), so it is meant for GPU-resident pipelines.
