`IcpPlaneCriteria::association` selects how `icp_projective_plane` reads the target at a source point's projection: `Association::Nearest` (the default, the previous behaviour), `Association::Bilinear`, a bilinear blend of the vertex, normal and sigma maps that removes the half-pixel snapping bias, or `Association::Hybrid`, nearest until the finest level's update falls below `HYBRID_SWITCH_NORM`, then bilinear. A bilinear sample reads only the pixels it weights and is dropped when one of them is invalid or they straddle a depth step larger than `MAX_BILINEAR_DEPTH_JUMP`.
