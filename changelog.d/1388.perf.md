CUDA `pyrup_u8` runs as one fused kernel instead of two passes through an intermediate buffer, and its adapter no longer allocates one per call. On an RTX 4050 Laptop GPU, a call (launch plus synchronize) takes 0.45–0.73× the time it did before: for example 3-channel 1920×1080 drops from 430 to 195 µs, and 1-channel 3840×2160 from 676 to 438 µs.
