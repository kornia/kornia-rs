`kornia_3d::pnp::refine_pose_lm` now works far from the world origin and in millimetres (#1202). Its `f32` solver lost the pose update in rounding when coordinates were large: with the scene 1000 m from the origin, a 0.5° start error only fell to 0.485°, and 271 of 5217 refinements on the EPOS PnP data of the 2020 RANSAC tutorial (millimetres) failed with "LU solve failed". It now solves in a frame centred on the half of the points nearest the start camera and scaled by the median distance of the points from that camera, then maps the pose back. EPnP with `refine_lm` (also the AP3P refit of `solve_pnp_ransac`) and the Python `solve_pnp_ransac` polish use it. `initial_lambda` and `gradient_tolerance` now act in that frame.
