`kornia_3d::stereo::SadRefine::centred` (default `true`) switches the sub-pixel SAD between ORB-SLAM3's centred cost `Σ |(L − L_centre) − (R − R_centre)|` and the plain `Σ |L − R|`. Centring cancels a brightness offset between the cameras but changes which matches survive and where they land, so a consumer can now measure it on its own data. The CUDA matcher honours the flag and stays bit-identical to the CPU path.
