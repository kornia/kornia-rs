`kornia_3d::registration::icp_projective_plane` solves degenerate geometry instead of failing: directions of `J^T W J` whose depth-scaled eigenvalue is at most `IcpPlaneCriteria::degeneracy_rtol` (default `DEFAULT_DEGENERACY_RTOL`, 1e-8) get a zero update and zero information, and the rest is solved by eigen pseudo-inverse. A single plane now converges along its three observed directions and reports the other three in `IcpPlaneResult::degenerate_directions` and `num_degenerate`, where it used to return `RgbdIcpError::SingularNormalEquations`. Full-rank data keep the Cholesky solve.
