`calc_optical_flow_pyr_lk` with `BorderMode::Clamp` or `BorderMode::Mirror` no longer reports status 1 for a
track that left the image: as in `cv::calcOpticalFlowPyrLK`, a point is lost when its window's floored top-left
corner leaves `[-win_size, size)` at the finest level. Tracking also skips pyramid levels no larger than the 21x21
window, as `cv::buildOpticalFlowPyramid` does; on small images those levels pushed border points off the image
(a point at (0, 65.5) on a 96x72 image now tracks to within 0.65 px of OpenCV instead of 15 px). With
`BorderMode::Reject`, a point whose window was not inside a coarser level, at the start or while iterating, was
lost even when it fit at full size; it now skips the rest of that level and is lost only at level 0, as with the
other modes. A NaN point or initial guess is now lost instead of returning a NaN position with status 1, also in
`calc_optical_flow_pyr_lk_u8`.
