The `kornia-rs` wheels now require Python 3.9 or newer, and free-threaded 3.13t wheels are no longer built (3.14t wheels continue). The bindings moved to pyo3 0.30, numpy 0.30 and dlpack-rs 0.4; the `dlpack` feature of `kornia-tensor` and `kornia-image` now depends on dlpack-rs 0.4. To migrate, use Python 3.9+ or the 3.14t free-threaded build, and move any crate that shares DLPack types with kornia to dlpack-rs 0.4.
