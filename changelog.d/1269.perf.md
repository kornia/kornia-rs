Descriptor matching is faster, with the same pairs. On an i7-14700K: SIFT `match_descriptors` is 81-108x faster on x86_64 (4,096 descriptors, cross-check, 1 thread: 7.5 s to 71 ms), because `f32::mul_add` was a software call there and the pass now runs in AVX2+FMA tiles; `match_descriptors_f32` is 7-11x faster; Hamming `match_descriptors` with `cross_check` and a ratio is 20-29% faster above 512 descriptors at 4 threads, through POPCNT. On an RTX 4090, `SiftMatcher` cross-checks 2x faster at 4,096 descriptors and 3.5x at 8,192. aarch64 runtime is unmeasured.
