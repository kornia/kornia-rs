`UmeyamaError` gains `InvalidDelta`, `InvalidMaxIters`, and `InvalidTol`
(reported for invalid `RobustUmeyamaConfig` fields); it is not
`#[non_exhaustive]`, so exhaustive downstream `match`es need three new arms.

`UmeyamaOutputF64` is now a struct with `r`, `t`, and `s` fields instead of a
`(R, t, s)` tuple; update destructuring and `.0`/`.1`/`.2` accesses. The f32
`UmeyamaOutput` is unchanged.
