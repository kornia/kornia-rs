`kornia_algebra::simd::F32x8` and `Mask32x8` are one 256-bit AVX register when x86-64 is built with the `avx2` target feature (for example `-C target-cpu=x86-64-v3`); other builds keep the two-`F32x4` path. The choice is made at compile time, and both paths give bit-identical results. On `bench_simd_lanes` (ICP association, 19,200 points, one core), `F32x8` drops from 24.7 to 15.4 ns/point with `+avx2,+fma` (1.6x), level with `wide::f32x8` (15.6 ns/point).
