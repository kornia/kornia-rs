`calc_optical_flow_pyr_lk` with `BorderMode::Clamp` (the default) samples a window that crosses the image border from a per-window table, four lanes at a time on `kornia_algebra::simd::F32x4`, instead of one bilinear sample per pixel. 200 points within 4 px of the border of a 256x256 image track in 10.3 ms instead of 22.2 ms (2.2x; x86-64, one core, best of 5 interleaved runs). Coarse pyramid levels send most windows through this path, so interior points speed up too: 200 points on a 256x256 image, 33.1 ms to 13.7 ms.
