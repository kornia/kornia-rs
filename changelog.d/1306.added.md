`IcpPlaneResult` now reports how far to trust a pose, all from a final pass at the returned pose: `num_associated`, `inlier_fraction` (inliers over associated pixels, match quality alone), `overlap_fraction` (associated over valid source pixels, the cue to pick a new target frame), `rotation_conditioning` and `translation_conditioning` (weakest over strongest Cholesky pivot of each 3x3 block of `J^T W J`, independent of the correspondence count), `observability` (the weaker block, a single gate for geometry such as a wall that fills the view) and `median_depth_m`.
