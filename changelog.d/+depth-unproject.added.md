`kornia_3d::depth`: `unproject_depth` / `unproject_depth_into` turn a `u16` or `f32` depth image (scaled by a `metres_per_unit` multiplier) and `PinholeCamera` intrinsics into a `PointCloud`, skipping masked pixels and missing samples (same rule as `kornia_imgproc::depth`: `u16` valid iff non-zero, `f32` valid iff finite and positive), optionally with each point's source pixel index; the `_into` form reuses the caller's buffers. `metres_per_unit` must be finite and positive (subnormals included); for `u16`, `65535 · metres_per_unit` must not overflow. Checked against Open3D's `create_from_depth_image`.
