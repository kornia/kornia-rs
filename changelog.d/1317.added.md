`kornia_3d::registration::icp_projective_plane` solves degenerate geometry instead of failing: directions of `J^T W J` whose depth-scaled eigenvalue is at most `IcpPlaneCriteria::degeneracy_rtol` (default `DEFAULT_DEGENERACY_RTOL`, 1e-8) get a zero update and zero information, and the rest is solved by eigen pseudo-inverse. A single plane now converges along its three observed directions and reports the other three in `IcpPlaneResult::degenerate_directions` and `num_degenerate`, where it used to return `RgbdIcpError::SingularNormalEquations`. Full-rank data keep the Cholesky solve.

`IcpPlaneCriteria::rotation_prior` takes an optional `RotationPrior`, an external estimate of the source-to-target rotation (e.g. integrated gyro rates) with a one-sigma error `sigma_rad`. It enters every Gauss-Newton step of `icp_projective_plane` as three rows weighted `1 / sigma_rad^2`, added after the directions the data do not observe are removed, so it carries a rotation the geometry cannot see (a roll-invariant scene) and is outvoted where the geometry is rich. The reported conditioning, observability, degenerate directions and `information` stay data-only. A `sigma_rad` that is not finite and positive, or a prior rotation that is not a finite rotation matrix, is reported as `RgbdIcpError::InvalidCriteria`.

`icp_projective_plane` takes an optional 6-DoF pose prior, `IcpPlaneCriteria::pose_prior`: a rotation, a translation and a 6x6 covariance in the solver's `[w, v]` tangent, for instance the motion predicted by a fused odometry. It enters each Gauss-Newton step as six rows weighted by the inverse covariance, after the directions the depth data do not observe are removed, so it carries the slide along a bare wall while the observed directions still follow the data. It adds to a rotation prior if both are set, never enters the reported information, conditioning or degenerate directions, and an invalid rotation, translation or covariance (not finite, symmetric and positive definite) is an `InvalidCriteria` error.

`IcpPlaneCriteria::association` selects how `icp_projective_plane` reads the target at a source point's projection: `Association::Nearest` (the default, the previous behaviour), `Association::Bilinear`, a bilinear blend of the vertex, normal and sigma maps that removes the half-pixel snapping bias, or `Association::Hybrid`, nearest until the finest level's update falls below `HYBRID_SWITCH_NORM`, then bilinear. A bilinear sample reads only the pixels it weights and is dropped when one of them is invalid or they straddle a depth step larger than `MAX_BILINEAR_DEPTH_JUMP`.
