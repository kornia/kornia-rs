`IcpPlaneResult` now reports how far to trust a pose, all from a final pass at the returned pose: `num_associated`, `inlier_fraction` (inliers over associated pixels, match quality alone), `overlap_fraction` (associated over valid source pixels, the cue to pick a new target frame), `rotation_conditioning` and `translation_conditioning` (weakest over strongest Cholesky pivot of each 3x3 block of `J^T W J`, independent of the correspondence count), `observability` (the weaker block, a single gate for geometry such as a wall that fills the view) and `median_depth_m`.

RGB-D pyramids now accept metre depth as `Image<f32, 1>`: `vertex_map_from_depth_m`, `downsample_depth_half_m` and `RgbdPyramid::from_depth_m` mirror their millimetre counterparts without the millimetre quantisation, and treat `0`, negative and non-finite samples as invalid, the rule of `kornia_imgproc::depth::is_valid_depth_f32`.

`icp_projective_plane` weights each row by a depth noise model, `IcpPlaneCriteria::depth_noise`: `DepthNoise::Constant` (the previous uniform weighting, which replaces the `sigma_m` field) or `DepthNoise::Stereo`, whose depth sigma grows with `z^2`, projected onto the target normal along both points' rays. `RgbdPyramid::from_depth_m_with_sigma` carries a per-pixel depth sigma map into every level (`RgbdLevel::depth_sigmas`), and that map takes precedence over the model. Invalid noise parameters and a sigma map of the wrong size are reported as errors.

`IcpPlaneResult::information` reports the 6x6 information matrix `J^T W J` of `icp_projective_plane` at the returned pose: the data term only, in the solver's left tangent `[w, v]` (radians, then metres, target frame). Under the depth noise model its inverse is the covariance of the pose estimate.
