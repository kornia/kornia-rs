`kornia_3d::pointcloud::filter`: point cloud outlier filters. `remove_radius_outliers` and `remove_statistical_outliers` (with `*_inlier_indices` forms) follow PCL's radius and statistical outlier removal, searched on a uniform cell grid (a cell list, with the `kiddo` k-d tree as fallback) and parallel over the cells. As in PCL, non-finite points (NaN or infinite coordinates) are never inliers, never count as neighbours and are left out of the statistical filter's global mean and deviation; the grid search runs on the remaining finite points. `OutlierFilter` owns the search scratch and reuses it across calls (`radius_inlier_indices_into` / `statistical_inlier_indices_into`), so repeated filtering does not reallocate it.
