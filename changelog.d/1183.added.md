SQPnP pose solver in `kornia-3d`: `kornia_3d::pnp::sqpnp::solve_sqpnp`, also as `PnPMethod::SQPnP` / `PnPMethod::SQPnPDefault` in `solve_pnp` and `solve_pnp_ransac` (Terzakis & Lourakis, ECCV 2020; Rust only for now, the Python bindings still offer EPnP and AP3P). It minimises the algebraic reprojection error over rotations, as OpenCV's `SOLVEPNP_SQPNP`, for planar and non-planar points, and `SQPnPParams::refine_lm` can polish the pose on the reprojection error. With 1 px noise it failed (rotation error above 5°) on 0 of 200 20-point planar scenes and 1 of 200 4-point scenes; EPnP with LM failed on 49 and 23 (EPnP still has #1181). Tables in `crates/kornia-3d/benchmarks.md`. Inside `solve_pnp_ransac`, which scores pinhole residuals, an SQPnP kernel needs undistorted points and `distortion = None`, like AP3P.

`kornia-algebra`: `Mat3F64::nearest_rotation` and `SO3F64::from_matrix_nearest`, the rotation nearest to a 3x3 matrix, plus `From` conversions between `Mat3F64` / `Vec3F64` and nalgebra's `Matrix3<f64>` / `Vector3<f64>`, exact `f32 → f64` `From` for `Mat3AF32`, `Vec3AF32` and `Vec2F32`, and the rounding `Mat3F64::as_mat3af32` / `Vec3F64::as_vec3af32`.
