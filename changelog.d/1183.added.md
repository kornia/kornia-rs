SQPnP pose solver in `kornia-3d` (Terzakis & Lourakis, ECCV 2020): `kornia_3d::pnp::sqpnp::solve_sqpnp`, also as `PnPMethod::SQPnP` / `PnPMethod::SQPnPDefault` in `solve_pnp` and `solve_pnp_ransac`. It minimises the algebraic reprojection error over rotations, as OpenCV's `SOLVEPNP_SQPNP` does, for planar and non-planar points, and `SQPnPParams::refine_lm` can polish the pose on the reprojection error. With 1 px noise it failed (rotation error above 5°) on 0 of 200 20-point planar scenes and on 1 of 200 4-point scenes, where EPnP with LM failed on 106 and 27. As a `solve_pnp_ransac` kernel it costs about 12 times as much as EPnP and 15 times as much as AP3P per run. Tables in `crates/kornia-3d/benchmarks.md`. Rust only for now: the Python bindings still offer EPnP and AP3P. Inside `solve_pnp_ransac`, which scores pinhole residuals, an SQPnP kernel needs undistorted points and `distortion = None`, like AP3P.
