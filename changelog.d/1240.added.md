`kornia_imgproc::depth`: `median_blur_depth`, a 3×3 / 5×5 median blur for `u16` and `f32` depth images that takes the median of the valid samples only, on CPU and, with the `cuda` feature, on device-resident images (bit-identical to the CPU). A `u16` sample is valid iff it is non-zero, an `f32` sample iff it is finite and `> 0` (`0.0`, negative, `±inf` and NaN are all missing); the rule is public as `is_valid_depth_u16` / `is_valid_depth_f32`. A window with no valid sample writes `0` (`u16`) or NaN (`f32`). On images with no invalid sample the output equals `cv2.medianBlur`.
