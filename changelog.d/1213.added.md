`umeyama_f64_robust` fits `(R, t, s)` to point sets contaminated by gross
outliers: a Huber-weighted IRLS loop over `umeyama_f64` down-weights large
residuals, a one-sided MAD hard-rejection pass (`3.5 · MAD / Φ⁻¹(3/4)`, i.e.
≈ `5.19 · MAD`, measured from the median residual but rejecting only the high
side) drops them before a final unweighted refit on the survivors, and a
`with_scale` flag selects Sim3 (estimated scale) or rigid (`s = 1.0`)
alignment. When the MAD is exactly zero (noiseless inliers), correspondences at
or below the median residual are kept, so noise-free data with outliers
recovers exactly; the refit needs at least three survivors. The fit is
deterministic; `RobustUmeyamaConfig` controls the Huber threshold, iteration
cap, and convergence tolerance.
