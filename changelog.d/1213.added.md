`umeyama_f64_robust` fits `(R, t, s)` to point sets contaminated by gross
outliers: a Huber-weighted IRLS loop over `umeyama_f64` down-weights large
residuals, a MAD hard-rejection pass (`3.5 · MAD / Φ⁻¹(3/4)`, i.e. ≈
`5.19 · MAD`, around the median residual) drops them before a final unweighted
refit on the survivors, and a `with_scale` flag selects Sim3 (estimated scale)
or rigid (`s = 1.0`) alignment. When the MAD is exactly zero (noiseless
inliers), exact-zero-deviation correspondences are kept, so noise-free data
with outliers recovers exactly; the refit needs at least three survivors. The
fit is deterministic; `RobustUmeyamaConfig` controls the Huber threshold,
iteration cap, and convergence tolerance.
