Fixed `svd3_f64` accuracy depending on the scale and rank of the input. Its Jacobi sweeps and QR step used absolute thresholds, and it ran Jacobi on (AᵀA)² instead of AᵀA, so matrices with entries around 1e-2 or smaller came back with a wrong decomposition, rank-2 matrices were only accurate to ~1e-2 even at unit scale, and `umeyama_f64` returned rotations up to ~28° off for noise-free point clouds a few centimetres across. All thresholds are now relative or exact-zero tests and Jacobi runs on AᵀA, so on random full-rank and rank-deficient matrices from 1e-150 to 1e150 the reconstruction error and the off-diagonal of S stay at ~1e-14 (worst 3.1e-14) relative to the norm of the input (singular values are accurate relative to the largest one, not each to its own size). A dense 3×3 SVD costs ~11% more.
