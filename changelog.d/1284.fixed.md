Fixed `svd3_f64` accuracy depending on the scale of the input. Its Jacobi sweeps stopped on absolute thresholds, so matrices with entries around 1e-2 or smaller came back with a wrong decomposition, and `umeyama_f64` returned rotations up to ~28° off for noise-free point clouds a few centimetres across. The stopping and skip tests are now relative to the matrix, so inputs from 1e-13 to 1e50 get the accuracy that inputs around 1e3 and above already had; inputs at or below ~1e-15 remain limited by the QR step's absolute threshold. A dense 3×3 SVD costs ~9% more (one more sweep).
