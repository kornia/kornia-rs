Fixed `svd3_f64` accuracy depending on the scale and rank of the input. Its Jacobi sweeps and QR step used absolute thresholds, and it ran Jacobi on (AᵀA)² instead of AᵀA, so matrices with entries around 1e-2 or smaller came back with a wrong decomposition, rank-2 matrices were only accurate to ~1e-2 even at unit scale, and `umeyama_f64` returned rotations up to ~28° off for noise-free point clouds a few centimetres across. All thresholds are now relative or exact-zero tests and Jacobi runs on AᵀA. On random well-conditioned full-rank and rank-deficient matrices from 1e-150 to 1e150, the reconstruction error and the off-diagonal of S stay at ~1e-14 (worst 3.1e-14) relative to the norm of the input (singular values are accurate relative to the largest one, not each to its own size). The error grows as σ2/σ1 shrinks, because AᵀA squares the singular values: worst over 5000 random rotations, ~1e-12 at σ2/σ1 = 1e-3, ~3e-10 at 1e-6 and ~1e-8 at 1e-7. Inputs with entries near the subnormal range no longer return NaN. A dense 3×3 SVD costs ~11% more.
