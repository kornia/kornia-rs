Fixed `svd3_f64` accuracy depending on the scale and rank of the input. Its Jacobi sweeps and QR step used absolute thresholds, and it ran Jacobi on (AᵀA)² instead of AᵀA, so matrices with entries around 1e-2 or smaller came back with a wrong decomposition, rank-2 matrices were only accurate to ~1e-2 even at unit scale, and `umeyama_f64` returned rotations up to ~28° off for noise-free point clouds a few centimetres across. All thresholds are now relative or exact-zero tests and Jacobi runs on AᵀA, so the decomposition is accurate to ~1e-14 for full-rank and rank-deficient inputs from 1e-150 to 1e150 (measured on random matrices). A dense 3×3 SVD costs ~11% more.
