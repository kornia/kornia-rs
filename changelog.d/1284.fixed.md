Fixed `svd3_f64` accuracy depending on the scale of the input. Its Jacobi sweeps stopped on absolute thresholds, so matrices with entries around 1e-2 or smaller came back with a wrong decomposition, and `umeyama_f64` returned rotations up to ~28° off for noise-free point clouds a few centimetres across. The stopping and skip tests are now relative to the matrix; results are accurate to ~1e-14 from 1e-15 to 1e50, at ~9% extra cost on a dense 3×3 SVD.
