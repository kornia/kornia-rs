`icp_projective_plane` takes an optional 6-DoF pose prior, `IcpPlaneCriteria::pose_prior`: a rotation, a translation and a 6x6 covariance in the solver's `[w, v]` tangent, for instance the motion predicted by a fused odometry. It enters each Gauss-Newton step as six rows weighted by the inverse covariance, after the directions the depth data do not observe are removed, so it carries the slide along a bare wall while the observed directions still follow the data. It adds to a rotation prior if both are set, never enters the reported information, conditioning or degenerate directions, and an invalid rotation, translation or covariance (not finite, symmetric and positive definite) is an `InvalidCriteria` error.
