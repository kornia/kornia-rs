`OrbScratch` and `OrbDetector::detect_and_extract_u8_into`: ORB-SLAM3-mode ORB on the calling thread with caller-owned working memory and output, the same bits as `detect_and_extract_u8` and no heap allocation once reused (was 8395 allocations, 14.3 MB per 752x480 frame).
`OrbCvDetector` and `OrbCvScratch`: OpenCV-compatible ORB returning `OrbFeatures`, with the keypoints, angles and descriptors of `cv::ORB::create()` bit for bit on the EuRoC fixtures (OpenCV 5.0), allocation-free with a reused scratch, and faster than `cv::ORB` on one thread.
