`kornia_3d::pose::Pose3d::orthonormalized` now uses `Mat3F64::nearest_rotation`. For a well-conditioned matrix it returns the nearest rotation to rounding error. `svd3_f64` was off by more than 1e-6 on about 1 in 100 random 3x3 matrices, by over 1e-2 on rare ones, and by up to 4e-7 near a rotation. When the second singular value is much smaller than the largest, accuracy drops (see `Mat3F64::nearest_rotation`). A rotation scaled by a large factor (from about 1e78 for a general rotation) no longer trips a debug assertion in `svd3_f64`.
