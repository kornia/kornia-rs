`IcpPlaneCriteria::rotation_prior` takes an optional `RotationPrior`, an external estimate of the source-to-target rotation (e.g. integrated gyro rates) with a one-sigma error `sigma_rad`. It enters every Gauss-Newton step of `icp_projective_plane` as three rows weighted `1 / sigma_rad^2`, added after the directions the data do not observe are removed, so it carries a rotation the geometry cannot see (a roll-invariant scene) and is outvoted where the geometry is rich. The reported conditioning, observability, degenerate directions and `information` stay data-only. A `sigma_rad` that is not finite and positive, or a prior rotation that is not a finite rotation matrix, is reported as `RgbdIcpError::InvalidCriteria`.
