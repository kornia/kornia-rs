`pyrup_u8` (CPU and CUDA, and `kornia_rs.imgproc.pyrup` on uint8 images) now equals OpenCV's `cv2.pyrUp` byte for byte. It used `reflect_101` neighbours at the last column and row, where `pyrup_f32` and OpenCV weight the edge pixel 1-7 and repeat it, so the right and bottom borders were off by up to about 100 levels; and it rounded after each pass, which left interior pixels one level off. Outputs of `pyrup_u8` change accordingly.
