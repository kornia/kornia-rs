`kornia_3d::camera::CameraModel`: one projection interface for all camera models. `project` maps a camera-frame point to a pixel in the raw (distorted) image, `project_with_jacobian` also returns the rows of `∂(u, v)/∂p_cam`, and `unproject` returns a unit bearing. `PinholeCamera` (now applying its Brown-Conrady distortion on projection) and `FisheyeCamera` (with an analytic Kannala-Brandt Jacobian) implement it, and the `Camera` enum (`Pinhole` / `Fisheye`) selects the model at runtime. `pose::triangulate_matched_points` and `TwoViewResult::median_parallax_deg` are generic over the model, so fisheye rays past 90° now triangulate.
