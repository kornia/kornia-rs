`kornia_3d::registration::icp_projective_plane` estimates the rigid motion between two `RgbdPyramid`s by projective point-to-plane ICP, coarse to fine with Gauss-Newton and Huber weighting. `IcpPlaneCriteria` sets its gates and iteration budget, and `IcpPlaneResult` returns the `T_target_source` rotation and translation with the residual and inlier count at that pose. Degenerate geometry, too few correspondences and invalid parameters are reported as new `RgbdIcpError` variants.
