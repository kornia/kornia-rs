`SE3F32::left_jacobian` and `SE3F32::right_jacobian` are now correct for every
rotation. One coefficient of their `Q` block divided by θ⁵ twice and the
small-angle series had the wrong terms, so the error grew as the rotation
shrank: 0.24 at 1.2 rad, 12 at 0.1 rad and 3.5·10⁴ at 3 mrad, the size of an
optimizer's increments.
