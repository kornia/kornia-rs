`kornia_algebra::simd` adds `F32x4` and `Mask32x4`, four-lane `f32` and mask types on glam's `Vec4`/`BVec4A` (SSE2 on x86-64, NEON on aarch64) for structure-of-arrays kernels: arithmetic, `mul_add`, `sqrt`, `abs`, `min`/`max`, slice load/store, ordered comparisons to masks (`false` on NaN), mask `&`/`|`/`!`, `select`, `element_sum`, `any`/`all` and `bitmask`. `min`/`max` return the right-hand operand when either lane is NaN on every target. A criterion bench, `bench_simd_lanes`, compares it with `wide::f32x8` on a point-to-plane ICP association pass.
