`RobustKernel::rho(r_sq, c_sq)` returns the robust objective whose derivative
is the IRLS weight (`ρ(0) = 0`, `dρ/ds = weight`), so a least-squares solver
can accept or reject steps on the cost `½ρ(s)` that matches its weights
(`½·w·s` is not that cost). Implemented for `IdentityKernel` (`s`),
`HuberKernel` (`s`, then `2√(c² s) − c²` past the knee), `CauchyKernel`
(`c² ln(1 + s/c²)`), `TukeyKernel` (`(c²/3)(1 − (1 − s/c²)³)`, then `c²/3`)
and `RobustKernelKind`. A degenerate (`c² ≤ 0`) or infinite scale gives `s`.
