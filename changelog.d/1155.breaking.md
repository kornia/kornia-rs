`kornia_3d::pnp::refine_pose_lm`, `refine_pose_lm_analytic`, and `refine_pose_lm_analytic_supports` now always reject unsupported robust kernels regardless of `LMRefineParams::robust_scale_sq`: `RobustKernelKind::Tukey` returns an error in both solvers, and `RobustKernelKind::Cauchy` is rejected by `refine_pose_lm_analytic` / `refine_pose_lm_analytic_supports`, even when `robust_scale_sq` is non-finite (`f32::INFINITY`, `NaN`), which previously fell back to plain L2. For supported kernels (`Huber`, `Cauchy`), `robust_scale_sq <= 0.0` (including `0.0`, which previously errored) and subnormal scales (`< f32::MIN_POSITIVE`) now silently fall back to plain L2.
