`kornia_3d::pnp::refine_pose_lm` (and `refine_pose_lm_analytic` for `Huber`) now falls back to plain L2 when `LMRefineParams::robust_scale_sq` is non-finite, non-positive (`<= 0.0`), or subnormal (`< f32::MIN_POSITIVE`) for supported robust kernels (`Huber` and `Cauchy` in `refine_pose_lm`; `Huber` in `refine_pose_lm_analytic`), instead of taking the square root of a negative scale (producing `NaN` weights) or underflowing to zero inside the loss.
