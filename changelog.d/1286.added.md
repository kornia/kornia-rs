`kornia_3d::registration`: projective point-to-plane ICP for depth-camera odometry (`icp_projective_plane`), with an RGB-D pyramid (`RgbdPyramid`, `DepthIntrinsics`), per-pixel depth-noise weighting, rotation and 6-DoF pose priors, and quality outputs (inlier and overlap fractions, conditioning, a data-only information matrix). Allocation-free after warm-up via `IcpWorkspace`, with SIMD kernels (`wide`) and a `PreparedTarget` keyframe cache. Level 0 is limited to 160x100; use `halvings_to_fit` to decimate larger depth maps.
