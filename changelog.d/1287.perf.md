`kornia-imgproc` frontend kernels are faster with bit-identical results: `pyrdown_f32` on single-channel images 2.2x and single-channel Scharr gradients 2.5x over 4 pyramid levels (lane-wise SIMD), and `calc_optical_flow_pyr_lk` 2.0x on 1415 points (2.7x on 365 border points) from tabulated border windows. Measured on 752x480 EuRoC frames. Also adds `detect_and_extract_u8_from_pyramid`, which runs ORB on a caller-built pyramid so a tracker can share the one it already has.
