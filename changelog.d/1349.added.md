`kornia_algebra::SMatF64<R, C>` and `SVecF64<N>`: fixed-size f64 matrix and vector types (nalgebra-backed, on the stack) for sizes past glam's 4x4, with `Mat9F64`/`Vec9F64` for an IMU navigation state (rotation, velocity, position) and `Mat15F64`/`Vec15F64` for that state plus gyroscope and accelerometer biases. `Mat3F64` and `Vec3F64` convert to and from `nalgebra::Matrix3<f64>` and `nalgebra::Vector3<f64>` with `From`, e.g. to write a rotation block into a larger Jacobian.
