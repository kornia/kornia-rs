`svd3_f32` now returns a diagonal `S` holding the actual singular values. Its xz Jacobi step rotated the matrix one way and the accumulated rotation the other, so `V` did not diagonalize `AᵀA` and the smaller singular values could be far off. `svd3_f64` was not affected. Results built on `svd3_f32`, such as EPnP, can change slightly.
