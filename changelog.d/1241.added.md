`kornia_imgproc::depth`: flying-pixel detection and removal for `u16` and `f32` depth images, on CPU and, with the `cuda` feature, on device-resident images (bit-identical to the CPU), under the same validity rule as `median_blur_depth` (`is_valid_depth_u16` / `is_valid_depth_f32`).
- `flying_pixel_mask` — flags valid pixels farther than `max(abs_threshold, rel_threshold * d)` from the median of the valid samples in their 3×3 window, or with fewer than `min_valid` valid taps; invalid pixels are never flagged.
- `invalidate_masked_depth` — writes the invalid marker (`0` for `u16`, NaN for `f32`) where the mask is set and copies every other pixel unchanged.
