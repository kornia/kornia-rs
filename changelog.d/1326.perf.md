ORB (`OrbDetector::detect_and_extract_u8`) on a baseline x86-64 build rotates the BRIEF pattern in an AVX2 copy when the CPU has it (the `round_ties_even` calls were libm calls without SSE4.1), and single-channel bilinear `resize_fast_u8` has an SSE2 row kernel; both give the same bytes. On a 752x480 EuRoC frame with 1000 keypoints, one thread: 53.6 -> 29.5 ms at x86-64 and 32.1 -> 28.6 ms at x86-64-v3 (`cv::ORB` 34.6 / 32.3 ms).
