`kornia_3d::pose::Pose3d` adds rigid-transform and representation helpers: conversions to and from `glam::DAffine3`, `kornia_algebra::Mat4F64`, and `kornia_algebra::SE3F64`; `from_quat_translation` (with quaternion normalization) and `to_quat_translation`; `yaw` heading extraction about +Z (`atan2(R[1][0], R[0][0])`); `planar` ground-plane projection; and `rotation_angle` geodesic distance using the numerically stable quaternion formula `2 * atan2(||v||, |w|)`.
