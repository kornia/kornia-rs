`kornia_3d::pose::Pose3d` adds rigid-transform and representation helpers: `From` conversions with `glam::DAffine3`, `kornia_algebra::Mat4F64` (plus `from_matrix`/`to_matrix`), and `kornia_algebra::SE3F64` (`From<SE3F64>` and `to_se3`); `from_quat_translation` and `to_quat_translation`; `yaw` heading extraction in `[-π, π]` from `R_wc = R_cwᵀ` (with gimbal-lock fallback); `planar` ground-plane projection preserving camera center `(c_x, c_y, 0)`; and `rotation_angle` geodesic distance using `2 * atan2(||v||, |w|)` (with automatic `SO(3)` projection on drifted rotations across quaternion-extracting helpers).
